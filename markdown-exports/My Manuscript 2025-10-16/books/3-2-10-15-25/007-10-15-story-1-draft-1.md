# 10-15 Story 1 Draft 1

Celeste walked through the garden. The air was thick, almost dewy with the heavy floral scents. She felt the warm breeze as it wrapped and unwrapped her dress around the bottoms of her legs. The sunlight wrapped her with warmth.

This was the last place she thought she would find herself right now. Roger died just weeks ago. The plane crash, the search, it was all a blur. She casually picked an orange nasturtium and continued drifting around, tucking it's stem behind her ear.

She looked around, where did that dog go she thought, "Palto, come here!"

Celeste smiled as an overweight white retriever came out of what she assumed was a napping spot. Palto looked at her, yawned and stretched. he squinted his eyes, a smile on his face.

"Good boy," said Celeste, patting her leg. Palto ran up to her and pressed his head against her dress. Celeste ran her fingers through his soft coat. 

She watched as George pulled up in his SUV. She waved and George nodded his head. She walked towards him, reaching his car as he was stepping out.

"Hello Celeste, I'm so sorry, I've heard about everything that's going on." He hugged her and squeezed tight. "Any news?" he asked pulling away from the embrace. 

George reached down and gave Palto a scruffy rub down his back. Palto turned bumping his tail end into Georges leg, tail wagging.

"No," said Celeste. "They found the crash but there was no sign of Roger. they found this necklace in the wreckage." She reached into her purse and pulled out a silver necklace with a shining blue stone, it sparkled in the sunlight.

"That's odd, so, do you think he wasn't alone?" George's eyes had a gravity to them as he asked the question. 

"I don't know, I'd never seen this before they gave me the box of his belongings," Celeste put the necklace back in her purse. "I was hoping you'd have some clue's."

"I don't, I'm sorry, Roger and I haven't talked in a long time." George tightened his lips thinking. "This must be so difficult having no answers about Rogers whereabouts and this necklace, puzzling." He ran his hand on her shoulder, "I'll help any way I can." He took her hand and smiled.

"Thank you George," she said. They both pulled away, there hands slowly coming apart. 

George opened his door and put one leg in turning back to Celeste, "Really, if there's anything I can do." 

Celeste smiled and nodded her head before drifting back towards her car.
