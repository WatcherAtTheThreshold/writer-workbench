# 10-15 Story 3 Draft 1

Fern stood looking out at the green field, the tall grass blowing in the wind. Small barn swallows dipped and looped in the air. He scanned the tree line at the edge of the field. A large deer lifted it's head, they saw each other and it darted  into the woods.

Fern looked down the dusty road and continued walking, his cape swaying as he walked. His pack-roll and backpack tight against his back

He could see someone up ahead, standing on the side of the road, a long cape billowing in the wind. As he got closer they appeared to be standing still. He walked up and stood in front of it. It was a robot, unmoving, coated with layers of gray road dust. It's tattered cloak flapped about in the breeze.

"Hello?" he said, leaning towards the automaton, seeing if it could communicate. A swallow swooped close beside them.

Fern tapped the robots chest with his finger. A cloud of dust fell and was blown away in the wind.

He walked away from the defunct robot.  

The road cut into the forest. The trees canopy forming a cathedral like ceiling. sunlight spilled through the leaves dappling the road.

As soon as he entered the forest he noticed small cloaked figures darting behind trees keeping pace with him, he was being followed. He stopped, all of the watchers stopped as well. Fern took a few more steps, they started moving behind trees again. He stopped again and they stopped again. 

Fern put his hands on his hips and stood still looking around. After a minute he folded his arms on his chest and just stood there. He stood still and they stood still. 

"OK, you can come out," he said "I'm not moving until you do," Arms still folded, he cocked his head to the side and looked around. After a few minutes he gave up and just started walking again. 

As he drew deeper into the woods they seemed to be pushing ahead of him until he could no longer see them through the trees. Not much further down the road he could see what looked like chimney smoke rising into the air.

As he got closer he could see the village come into view, but it was a small village. The followers in the woods looked really tiny, this must be there homes. He walked up to the edge of town and called out "I don't want to cause alarm, can I pass through your town, or even better can I talk to one of you?" He stood, waiting.

"What do  you want to talk about," a voice called back from an unknown location.

"I'm looking for a cave, one that has crystals and old machines  and a titan", He looked around waiting for a response.

A small cloaked creature, no taller than knee high, walked into view. "This is our home, It's called Longbrook. You're welcome to stay in peace." She stepped closer to Fern and bowed her head, "I am Elžbieta."

"I am Fern, nice to meet you". Fern bent down and shook  Elžbieta's hand.

Others came out to get a look at Fern, they all looked similar. Almost mouse-like features, fur all over. Some wore cloaks others wore pants and shirts, dresses and blouses. They were murmuring and whispering about him. 

"I'm Fern," he said to the gathering crowd.

One of them stepped forward and said "I am Leva, if you are an adventurer we could use your help"
