# 10-15 Story 2 Draft 1

George sat up and rubbed his eyes. He pushed his legs off of the bed and stood, his legs aching. He rubbed his thighs and stiffly walked to the bathroom. He splashed water on his face and looked at himself in the mirror. His hair was messy, he combed through it with his fingers trying to tame the nest. He shaved off his thin mustache and his four four chin hairs.

"GEORGE!" called his older sister from downstairs. "We're going to be late, HURRY UP!"

Ignoring her he brushed his teeth, pausing to flex his biceps in the mirror. He smiled at himself and using his fingers he brushed his hair to the side again. He ran downstairs to catch the bus with both of his sisters. They were already standing by the door, coats on, backpacks on there backs.

George found his backpack, put his coat on and the three of them walked out to door. 

They walked into town, the morning air chilly on Denise and Karen's bare ankles. The bus was stopped by the common. A line of kids shuffled on through the open door. They got in line and hopped onto the bus.

Walking to the back of the bus Gerry Archibald put his arm across the aisle stopping George. "Nice hair Georgie," said Gerry antagonistically. Denise heard and rolled her eyes turning back towards the front of the bus. 

George leaned into Gerry's seat and said, "F@#k you, " a deadpan look in his eyes. Gerry's smile faded into anger, but before Gerry could respond Denise grabbed his arm and moved it out of the way. She snatched one of George's backpack straps and pulled him to the back of the bus. Gerry watched them go to the back, eyes angry slits. he cracked his knuckles, a bitter scowl on his face.

                                                       ***

George dozed through the morning classes. Mrs. Develifane smacked his hand with a ruler, startling George awake. Mr. Laplant Slammed a book down on his desk and said, "Do not sleep in my class, Mr. Hunt." George stayed awake until lunch.

The bell rang, the class filtered out into the hallway. George walked to the cafeteria and got a tray. Moving down the line a lunch lady gave him a scoop of corn, another put  a balogna and cheese sandwich on his tray. He picked up a milk and walked to his friends table. He pushed in between Kenny and John. Rich, Jeff and Ray sat across from them.

They all hunched over their lunch's eating and joking. They were all freshmen so none of them wanted to draw attention, none of them wanted to be there. But they laughed and jostled  nonetheless.

At the end of lunch, bell about to ring George noticed Gerry approaching their table. He leaned into the middle of all his friends and said, "Sh#t! Gerry want's to fight" Gerry always had a chip on his shoulder, everyone said his dad beats him up. For some reason, today on the bus he decided to focus on George and George wasn't having any of it. As Gerry walked up George ignored him, pretending not to notice him walking towards their table and around behind George. 

Gerry yelled "F@#K", as Kenny stuck out his leg and tripped him. He probably intended to say "F@#k you," to George and maybe push him. But he got tripped and yelled  a swear in the middle of the cafeteria while falling down. 

Mr. Edgars, their gym teacher, walked up and asked, "What was that Mr. Archibald!", in a stern voice.

"These guys tripped me," said Gerry standing and dusting himself off.

Mr Edgars said, "Stop your whining excuses. Get to the principles office," and he shoved Gerry towards the cafeteria door. He looked around at each of them shaking his head in disapproval.
