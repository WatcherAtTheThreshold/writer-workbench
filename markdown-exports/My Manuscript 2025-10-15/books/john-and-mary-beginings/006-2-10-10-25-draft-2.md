# #2 10-10-25 Draft 2

Carmen sat at a tall table in the window, just observing people, passersby on the sidewalk, the two barista's behind the counter, customers in line. 

The female barista blinked up at the man in line, her  validation. She had done the same with Carmen when he was ordering before. She'd looked at him flirtatiously and Carmen responded with warm eyes, she felt seen. Carmen recognized that someone had taught her that making herself smaller made her lovable. The customer responded by acting arrogant. Repeating his order in a stern voice, not because he didn't think she had heard him the first time, but seemingly to assert his dominance. In turn the she fawned, blinking her big eyes more. 

All the while the male barista, cleaning the espresso machine,  seemed perturbed by the charade.

Carmen could see through people. Not with X-ray vision, but he could guess the many branching paths that lead to the person they ended up being. He saw why they did the things they did, acted the ways they acted, felt the ways they felt. With one look, one interaction, he couldn't intuit exactly what happened to them. But his mind would reverse engineer the probable paths that formed them into who they are.

Carmen turned on his stool and returned his gaze to people walking by on the sidewalk. His laptop was open, cursor blinking on a blank screen. He wrote a line and erased it, closing the laptop. Drinking the last sip of his coffee, he stood and pushed the stool in. Sliding the laptop into his messenger bag he fastened the clasps before leaving the coffee shop.  

He needed his next story, the deadline was looming and he searched for an interesting twist. Standing on the sidewalk a woman walked right into him. He was jostled backwards almost tripping over the concrete step in front of the coffee shop. She stumbled backwards, Carmen reached out grabbing her arms, preventing them both from stumbling over. 

"I'm sorry" Carmen blurted out. "are you alright?"

"I'm OK, I'm so sorry" she said "I wasn't watching where I was going".
