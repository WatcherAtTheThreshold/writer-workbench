# #2 10-10-25 Draft 5

John sat at a tall table in the window, just observing people, the barista behind the counter, customers in line. 

The barista floated between the cash register and the coffee machines, taking and filling orders. John sat watching the interaction as customers came in and left with coffees and pastries in hand.  An older woman stepped up to order, she was very polite. Leaving a tip as she walked away, the barista lit up and smiled with genuine warmth. The next man in line had a brash demeanor and carried an air of superiority. The barista physically withdrew, not making eye contact, he shrank in his posture. The man seemed to contemplate leaving a tip, then pocketed his change and walked out.

For some reason John could see through people. Not with X-ray vision, but with an intuitive hunch about the many branching paths that make up a human life. He saw why they did the things they did, acted the ways they acted, felt the ways they felt. With one look, one interaction, he wouldn't intuit exactly what had happened to them. But his mind would reverse engineer the probable paths that formed them into who they were.

John turned on his stool and returned his gaze to people walking by on the sidewalk. His laptop was open, cursor blinking on a blank page. He wrote a few lines and erased them. Closing the laptop, he drank the last sip of his coffee, stood and pushed his stool in. Sliding the laptop into his messenger bag he walked out the door.  

John stumbled when he stepped off of the concrete stoop, bumping into a woman walking by, "I'm sorry" John blurted out. "are you alright?"

"I'm OK" she said, flustered, straightening the necklace that hung around her neck.

They both smiled, and gathered themselves, Mary fiddled with the necklace. When they looked up at each other John was struck by a sense of déjà vu, something in her smile and her eyes. John quickly realized that he couldn't read her. She didn't seem to be acting out some role. Maybe she was... just her. She noticed flour  specked on John's shirt.

"Baking?" she asked, raising her eyebrows.

"Pumpkin pie", he replied, with a smile. "It's on the counter at home, cooling right now." 

"You're just going to tell me about pie and not invite me over to have some?" She would never normally do this sort of thing, but something about his eyes, something about in his smile.

"I would love to invite you over!" John said, a shocked look on his face. "I'm John" he said, putting his hand out. 

"I'm Mary", she replied and shook Johns hand.

                                                                                                                ***

John unlocked the door and lead her into his apartment. He noticed Mary looking around, not being able to pick up on her subtleties he was glad he'd cleaned up and that it smelled like fresh pumpkin pie. John took her coat and hung it up by the door. He pulled out a chair offering her a seat at the table. Walking to the kitchen he put on a pot of coffee.

He asked, "How do you take your coffee?", while carefully slicing the pie.

"A bit of milk or 1/2 & 1/2 and no sugar, please", 

After serving pie and coffee John inquired, "Where are you from, I'm guessing East Coast".

"Way East, Northern New Hampshire, nearly Canada". She took another bite and briefly closed her eyes, "This is really, really good pie, by the way" 

"Wait," John sat up "New Hampshire, really? I grew up in Lancaster!"

Her fork stopped halfway to her mouth, "I'm from Colebrook". They looked at one another and they both laughed.

John talked with ease, enthusiastically gesturing with his hands. He mentioned that he was an artist and he described the many ways he expressed himself. Mary said she too was an artist and talked about her love of painting. She mentioned her job search briefly and they reminisced about growing up in rural New Hampshire. 

Finishing her coffee and pie, Mary set her mug down on the table.  "Thanks for having me over," she said, putting her coat on.

John stood and walked her to the door "This was fun, we should go out again, can I get your number", said John, opening the door.

"This was fun," and Mary told him her number, John typed it into his phone. "Message me, we'll make a plan", said Mary.

Smiling, with a funny flutter in his stomach, John watched her walk away down the narrow hallway. He couldn't 'read' her but he also couldn't remember ever feeling like this about someone.

                                                                                                                ***

The next morning John returned to the cafe. He sat down at a tall window seat. He immediately noticed a small flash on the sidewalk, a piece of metal maybe. It drew his attention to a small plant growing from a crack. No sooner had he noticed it then a man walked by, he was arguing with someone over a bluetooth earpiece in his ear. He passed and the small green leaves growing from the sidewalk were just gone.

John saw his painful inspiration and he started to type. This flurry lasted hours. Then Mary crossed his mind. He stopped typing and looked out the window. He smiled, laughing a little at the idea of her actually being there when he looked up. 

He swigged his coffee, looking out the window. He set the coffee down and was about to start typing again when someone touched his shoulder and a familiar voice said "Hi John, do you mind if I sit here?" Mary was there, she pointed to the stool beside him. 

He quickly moved his laptop over and made space. Mary sat down smoothing her dress on her legs. As soon as she sat down he noticed all these little beautiful pieces of her. The curve of her fingers when she . Her elegant muted french manicure. A tress of hair curled perfectly down the side of her face. 

Somehow he couldn't read her but now he realized that he could see her like he saw nature, infinite small pieces that come together to create a beautiful mystery.
