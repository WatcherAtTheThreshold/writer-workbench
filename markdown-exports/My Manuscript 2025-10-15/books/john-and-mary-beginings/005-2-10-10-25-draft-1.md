# #2 10-10-25 Draft 1

Carman could see through people. Not with X-ray vision, but he could guess the many branching paths that lead to the person they ended up being. He saw why they did the things they did, acted the ways they acted, felt the ways they felt. With one look, one interaction, he couldn't intuit exactly what happened to them. But his mind would reverse engineer the probable paths that formed them into who they are. 

This wasn't a superpower, most people just thought he was weird. Maybe because he could see people for who they were those who were damaged or broken were drawn to him. Partially because he was so nice, but he was nice because he understood what hurt them. He didn't blame people for who they'd become. Something in them recognized that he forgave them for what they'd done.

                                                                                                    ***

Carman looked out a large window, sipping his latte.  He sat on a bar stool at a tall table, feet propped on the bottom rung. He watched people pass by out on the sidewalk.  He enjoyed watching people from this sort of 'outside looking in' view, some happy, some angry, some starring down at their phones. 

His laptop was open, cursor blinking on a blank screen. He wrote a line and erased it, closing the laptop. Drinking the last sip of his coffee, he stood and pushed the stool in. Slidding the laptop into his messenger bag he  fastened the clasps before leaving the coffee shop.  

He needed his next story, the deadline was looming and he searched for an interesting twist. Standing on the sidewalk a woman walked right into him. He was jostled backwards almost tripping over the concrete step in front of the coffee shop. She stumbled backwards, Carmen reached out grabbing her arms, preventing them both from stumbling over. 

"I'm sorry" Carman blurted out. "are you alright?"

"I'm OK, I'm so sorry" she said "I wasn't watching where I was going".
