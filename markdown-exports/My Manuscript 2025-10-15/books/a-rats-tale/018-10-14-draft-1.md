# 10-14 Draft 1

The morning sunlight splintered through the foliage spilling slivers of gold from underbrush. A small bird was hopping and gliding about, jumping from small bushes, to the ground, under the bush. Then it  hopped back out, chirping and singing. It stood, chest held hi before seeing a bug in a sunspot and jumping to the ground. 

A long brown mink came sniffing along. It darted into a rat hole at the edge of the yard,  sliding out of the sunlight into the dark earth below. Popping out on the other side of the garden, it put it's head up to look around, sniffing the breeze, head bobbing. It ran from the garden towards the house, diving into a hole it disappeared under the foundation.

                                                                                                                            ***

Jack tightened his red cape then sharpened his sword, scraping the long wooden blade against the concrete pillar in his room. Jack could smell fresh wood as pieces of sawdust drifted to the ground.  After a few good scrapes he tested the tip with his finger, it was sharp. He strapped the sword to his back and headed out into the main corridor that lead into the burrow. He needed to meet up with the others. Padding along, the soil beneath his feet was soft and damp. It smelled of earth and wood and dust and stone; and rats. It also smelled of fear, anxious cry's could be heard from the tunnels ahead.

Jack sprinted and swerved navigating the tight corridors. He heard Lisa's squeak from somewhere to his left. Without slowing he took a hard left and ran towards her voice. He heard her again but now she was up in the house somewhere. Jack climbed a wooden beam and jumped onto a pipe. This lead to a small hole into the walls.

"Lisa!", called Jack. She squeaked a reply from up ahead and they ran to meet each other. 

"Jack! Whats going on?" She was panicked, eyes wide.

"Randy ran up to me terrified and said he saw a monster outside, a predator, he ran off to go tell the others."

"What, a monster?" asked Lisa with squinted eyes and a furrowed brow.

"I don't know what he saw, I sharpened my sword and came to find you." He put his arm around Lisa, she leaned into him putting her arms around his waist. She leaned her face into his chest, closing her eyes and breathing deep. "Randy said it was out front so we should probably just stay up here."

"What about the others Jack, we can't just hide if we could help." He shook his head yes and hugged her tight. Jack jumped onto the pipe, leaped to the beam and jumped to the ground. 

Lisa landed softly beside him. The burrow sounded frantic, he could hear shouting and running. He could hear the commotion but he'd only seen Lisa since Randy ran by. He looked at Lisa and smiled feeling a warm glow in his chest. She noticed him looking and she smiled bashfully.

"Let's go but we should stay kind of quite and we should stick together, if anything happens. Let's head for the Midden and see if anyone's there" Jack put his head down and started carefully walking forward. In near silence they plodded down the damp tunnels.

Jack heard footsteps moving towards them, they both froze. He looked back and forth contemplating if they should turn and run or if they should move ahead trying to find an offshoot before the footsteps were upon them. Then the running drew close, Jack squeaked defensively. 

"Jack?" a voice called from the dark. It was Willow, she sounded scared.

"Willow, it's me and Lisa. Do you know what's going on?" Jack listened, the din of squeaking had stopped replaced by loud, singular, scared yells.

"We need to move away from those noises." Another loud squeak made them all jump, this one seemed closer.

They pushed ahead, Willow falling in line behind Lisa. They branched down  few different  paths and came out in a large chamber, the Midden. Jack walked into the room but stopped, the girls froze as soon as they made it through the doorway. Sitting in front of the Middens treasure pile was Jeremiah. He looked cranky, maybe he was scared but he didn't show it. Didn't he hear the panicked yelling, thought Jack. Then with a feeling that loosened his bowels Jack realized that Jeremiah was taking advantage of the chaos, they were in trouble.

"Jack, I'm so glad you're here," said Jeremiah. His head down and shoulders up he stalked towards them. Jack stepped back raising his hands. The girls looked around contemplating if they should run. Jeremiah  pressed forward.

"RUN!" shouted Jack. Lisa and Willow ran away down the tunnel. Jeremiah pounced on Jack, pinning him to the ground. Jack struggled but Jeremiah was nearly twice his size. Jeremiah bit hard into Jacks shoulder, Jack screamed in pain. 

There was a loud thunk and Jeremiah fell limp to the floor. Lisa stood over Jack smiling down. She handed the large rock she was holding to Willow who tossed it onto the ground. Another loud squeak startled them and Jack winced at his shoulder.

"You're bleeding!" said Lisa, wrapping a handkerchief around the wound. Willow motioned ahead and Jack lead them off out of the midden away from squeaks of terror still moving around somewhere from the other side of the burrow.
