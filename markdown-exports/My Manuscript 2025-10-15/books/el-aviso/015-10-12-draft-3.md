# 10-12 Draft 3

Travis drove through the cold desert, the sun just beginning to peak over the mountaintops in the distance. He was going to Levi's and Carmen's houses, though he hadn't been there for ten years. He didn't know if they'd be home, but Carmen's mother Rosa should be. So he headed to Carmen's house first. 

Pulling off the main road he drove onto the dusty dirt path that lead to Carmen's house. His truck bumped and jostled over rocks and potholes. Cresting a rise a coyote stood in his way, glowing yellow eyes reflecting his headlights. It looked lanky, shoulders raised, head hanging low. It stared at him, panting, head bobbing rhythmically. It's breath visible in the cool morning air.  

Travis turned the radio down, the coyote perked its ears, lifting it's head, it ran into the desert. Travis glanced at the box laying in the passenger seat before lifting his foot off the brake. The truck slowly tugged forward. 

He rolled up to the house and shut off his engine. Stepping out he stretched and yawned. Small desert birds flew about singing their morning songs.

He got back into the truck, laying his seat as far back as it would go, and fell asleep.

He awoke to stifling heat. The bright desert sun blinding him. Blinking and tired Travis put on sunglasses and stepped from  the truck, the air smelled like campfire. He got the box from his passenger seat and walked towards the front door. He knocked but no one answered, a dog lazily barked from inside. He pictured an old dog willing to bark but not willing to stand up to do so, he chuckled. 

He set the box by the door and turned to leave.  But paused, changed his mind, and picked the box back up. He put it back in the passenger seat and shut the door. From the desert someone called his  name. He looked around, Carmen's mother coming into view. 

"Travis, how are you?" she asked, slightly lifting the front of her long dress as she walked towards him. 

"Hello Rosa, how are you, you look beautiful as ever", They smiled and hugged. Her perfume was soft, almost muted, but it stayed with him after she pulled away. Travis couldn't place the pleasant smell but he really liked it.

"Thank you", she blushed and smiled at him, a shine to her eyes. "Carmen isn't home. She's in the mountains with Levi, they've been gone for days." She lifted her chin, peering  off into the desert. "They should be home anytime, but maybe not", she laughed. "Come inside", she invited him. "I'm making lunch". Not waiting for a response she looped her arm through Travis's and walked him into the house. 

Two pots sat cooking on the big iron woodstove, Rosa stirred each one, steam rising into the air. As Travis had guessed she had an old white faced dog. A retriever lay on the floor in the corner. It wanted to bark again, lifting it's head preparing to stand, but it was still lazy. It closed it's eyes it went back to sleep. 

"This is Arlo", said Rosa noticing Travis looking at the dog.

"I heard him bark when I knocked on the door, I assumed he'd be an old guy", Travis smiled.

Rosa nodded, smiling and blinking, "yup, I don't let him wander far these days, too many coyotes about".

"I saw one not far down the road", Travis could smell the refried beans and the tortillas Rosa was cooking in a flat pan. His stomach rumbled.

 "Oh, I brought some gifts for everyone, I'll go get them." and he walked out the door. 

Travis was halfway to the truck when a coyote walked out from behind it. It turned it's head to look at Travis. He maintained eye contact, unsure what else to do. He stopped but then slowly continued forward assuming the coyote would move on, but it didn't, Travis stopped again. "Hmm..." Travis said aloud. "What are we doing?". The coyote looked unbothered but continued to stare, tongue out slightly, panting in the heat.

Travis started to turn back towards the house but as soon as he moved the coyote dug it's paws into the dry, hard ground, as if to run. Travis couldn't tell what that could mean so he stopped moving.   

The coyote started to walk away from him but diagonally and it semi-circled back getting between Travis and the house. Closer to the truck inched up to it and hopped in. Travis honked his horn and the coyote ran off behind the house.

Rosa stepped out of the door, "What are you out here honking about", she yelled to Travis who was sitting in his passenger seat awkwardly squished beside the box. "Are you out here scared and honking", they both laughed. Travis pictured himself as a frightened goose, honking in the yard.

He got out of the truck and looked around nervously, picking up the box before shutting the door. "That coyote was here", he began to explain.

"That coyote, the same one you saw earlier?" Rosa asked with a smile, clearly teasing him.

"Not the same one, well maybe, I don't know. It seemed to want to block my way" Travis said, heart still beating hard.

"So a coyote was out here playing football with you or something", She said laughing at him.

Travis was confused, he didn't know what the coyote was doing. The old dog slowly walked out of the house and barked at him, as if he'd just arrived, then turned, dismissively and walked back indoors. Rosa watched Arlo and laughed. 

"Burritos are ready" she said, raising her eyebrows and motioning towards the house. Travis's stomach rumbled again. They walked inside, Arlo lifted his head from his pillow in the corner.

                                                                                                                      ***  

They walked in Travis set the box on floor. From it he pulled out a bottle of wine, he handed it to Rosa, she read the label as she brought it to the kitchen. He pulled out a six pack of glass bottled sodas and walked it to the fridge. 

"Would you like one of these?" He asked Rosa, she nodded, he pulled two out and closed the fridge. Using a wall mounted bottle opener he opened them and handed her one. She took a swig and picked up her plate, Travis got his and they both walked into the living room to sit and eat.

"So what happened with your coyote outside?" Rosa asked, smirking.

"I don't know, it seemed like it was confronting me?" Travis ate another bite and drank a sip of soda. "I don't know how to  describe it. It stared at me. then walked around me until I honked at it".

"Maybe it doesn't like your car?" Rosa said, Travis couldn't tell if she was just picking on him again. "Maybe it was a Bruja transformed" she said, cocking her head knowingly, raising her hands in a 'who knows' gesture.

"why would a witch in the shape of a coyote confront me in your dooryard?" Travis joked, poking back at her teasing him.

"Maybe 'she' doesn't like your car?" Said Rosa playfully. Travis smiled but thought it was creepy, all the witch talk. 

He didn't believe in brujas, but he didn't like the idea of one stalking out by his truck nonetheless.  

Arlo walked up to Travis, slow and arthritic, to beg for his last bite. Travis looked at Rosa, motioning towards Arlo with his last bite. Rosa shook her head yes. Travis reached out his hand and the old dog carefully and gently took the last of burrito from his hand and returned to his corner pillow, swallowing the bite before laying back down. 

"Whatever it was it's strange that it was confronting you", said Rosa. This further unsettled Travis but when he looked up at her face she was wearing a huge grin. "You're not going to get scared and go back out and honk more are you?" She asked, laughing harder this time, she slapped her leg, clearly amused by her own joking. 

                                                                                                                      ***  

As the day waned Travis started to fidget and look out the windows periodically. "When are those two supposed to return?" he asked again, clearly distracted by the idea of witch's and rogue coyote's. 

"Thinking of honking again, Travis?" asked Rosa sarcastically. 

Her joking was starting to rub him the wrong way. Why is she teasing me so much, he thought. Does she wan me to leave? Is she mad that I didn't respond to her advances when I first got here? He practically grew up there, the Carmen, Levi, Travis trio. From when he was thirteen or fourteen until he was around seventeen he was at Rosa's house nearly everyday. Thinking about it now though, and somehow he missed it earlier, ten years ago Rosa was a much more frail woman. She didn't just seem to have not aged in the ten years he had been gone, she looked younger now then when he last saw her. The witch fear struck him again and he looked at Rosa. She looked right back and widened her eyes. Travis wanted to run away.

"You look really good Rosa, do you have some new routine or some new herbs you take?", hoping for some explanation.

"My routine", said Rosa, looking off for a second. "Hmph", she said sounding annoyed. "Well, I wake up in the morning", and in a matter of fact tone started listing her routine, "I stretch my back, I put on my shoes, I go to the bathroom, I brush my teeth, I walk Arlo, I eat breakfast, I brush my hair, I go for a walk, I eat lunch, I walk Arlo again..." I must have looked annoyed because she stopped and asked "do you want me to continue?" in an overly sweet voice like I was a child.

Travis was confused, I guess I could have been being rude, he thought. I couldn't remember what face I was making. "Oh, no" said Travis "I was just thinking about how late it's getting" Travis's face turned hot and red. "Please continue" he said.

In a dry tone she said "Well if you're thinking about other things I'll just keep my routine to myself", sounding unamused. 

"Oh, please continue", said Travis, now sure that he must have been rude. Probably making some dramatic face in his fearful rumination. Travis now embarrassed, forgot that he had been angry a few minutes ago. "Please continue", he said again, smiling at her.

Rosa continued, picking up in the same tone and exactly where she left off "I clean and do the dishes, I make my bed, I water the houseplants, I go to town and go shopping at the market, I come home, I walk Arlo again, I make dinner, I eat, I wash up, I go to bed." She paused, thinking, It seemed like she was seeing if she forgot anything. She sat up like she remembered something. "Oh yeah, I train my pet coyote to scare guests", she said in the same serious voice and laughed.

Travis' eyes narrowed, he was annoyed again. She was picking on him. "I should go", he said, standing up, looking around to see if he had things to gather.

"But what about Carmen and Levi, they should be back anytime now?" She smiled at him looking coquettish again.

Travis smiled uncomfortably, bright pink filling his cheeks. "No, I have to get back, I thought they might not be here, they didn't know I was coming, that's why I brought these gifts, to leave in case I didn't see them". Travis realized that he was rambling but his mind was foggy with stress and he didn't know what to say, he just wanted to leave. While he was rambling Rosa was circling him and now stood between him and the door.

                                                                                                                      ***  

Travis looked around the room as if he was just going to ignore Rosa. Her standing between him and the door was just too much for his mind to handle. He shuffled around clearing his throat, running his hands over what was in his pockets. A bead of sweat ran into his eye, wiping at it he noticed Rosa shaking her hips back and forth rhythmically. Travis relaxed, smiled and  looked at her face, she was snarling, teeth bared. As soon as their eyes met she pounced on him, grabbing him by the wrists she wrestled him to the ground. Travis struggled back and forth, trying to pull his wrists free. Rosa was too strong. She was squeezing his wrists so hard he thought they might break. He couldn't help it, his shot nerves gave out and he started crying. He couldn't remember feeling so helpless.

Rosa let go, Travis fell back and rolled to safety. He sat up, rubbing his wrists, crouching defensively. Rosa quietly walked over to him and softly rubbed a hand on his back. He flinched away and looked at her. She laid her hand gently on his arm. She was smiling sweetly, with a  warm shine in her eyes. Travis wanted to lean into her soft touch.

"Did I scare you?" Rosa asked, harsh and condescending. "Travis, it was just horsing around, just wrestling. Don't be dramatic!" She was getting heated, her eyes suspicious slits. Little white pinpricks of light reflected back from her inky, dark pupils. She glared at Travis with a deep look of contempt. Then she walked away inot the next room, Travis assumed her bedroom and she slammed the door.

As soon as she was gone Travis stood up, looking out the window it was getting dark. The sun was gone behind the mountains and stars were starting to come out. Travis heard a loud bang, sounded like his trucks hood closing. In the dim light he could see the hood was closed and sires were hanging out from underneath it. He ran out the door and three or four coyotes scattered into the desert scrub. He ran to his truck, hopping into the drivers seat. He put the key in the ignition and turned it but it did nothing. He popped the hood and ran to open it up. Wires hung out the from under the hood, lifting something had disconnected the terminals from the battery. 

He started to try and re-attach them but it was too dark and he thought he might need tools. Something growled from the darkness. he tried to get back in the truck but something else growled from that direction. A chaotic chorus of high-pitched yips, yaps, and barks started. It grew in intensity, there seemed to be a large pack right behind the truck. Travis couldn't see a thing in the darkness and ran scared, back into the house.

Rosa was there looking sweet, playing innocent. 

Travis was distraught, "There's a whole pack of coyotes out there, did you hear that? They were very aggressive", he said, looking around frantically.

"I made Carmen's bed up so you'd have a place to sleep for the night", said Rosa, changing the subject. 

"Didn't you hear me, I think they disconnected my trucks battery", He stopped, realizing coyotes couldn't do that. It had to have been a human. He looked at Rosa. 

She reached out touching his arm. She pulled Travis close to her. His heart started beating so hard he thought she might hear it. Travis was scared in every way possible, to scared to react at all. 

She walked him into Carmen's room, sat him on the edge of the bed. "OK, you can sleep here", she said. She left the room and returned with a folded up towel, setting it beside Travis. "This is for washing up, in case you want to take a shower".

Travis, bewildered by the days events said, "Thank you", Rosa smiled and rubbed a hand on his back. Tired Travis closed his eyes, letting his head fall forward he nodded off to sleep.
