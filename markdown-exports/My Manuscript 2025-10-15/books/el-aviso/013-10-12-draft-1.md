# 10-12 Draft 1

Waking up before sunrise, John yawned and rubbed his eyes. Carmen sent a message to the group chat. Johns phone buzzed, an image of a rooster and the Bugle call of Reveille started playing. John smiled and got dressed. He checked his backpack and loaded it into the car.  Coffee in hand he drove to Levon's house, the sun just starting to light the sky.

After a short drive John pulled in, Levon and Carmen were waiting in the driveway.  John loaded his backpack into Levon's truck with the others. They sipped their coffee's. Levon unfolded the map and laid it out onto the hood of John's car.
