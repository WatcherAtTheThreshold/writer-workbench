# 10-13-Draft 4

Travis drove through the cold desert, the sun just beginning to peak over the mountaintops in the distance. He was going to Levi's and Carmen's houses, though he hadn't been there for ten years. He didn't know if they'd be home, but Carmen's mother Rosa should be. So, he headed to Carmen's house first.  

Pulling off the main road he drove onto the dusty dirt path that led to Carmen's house. His truck bumped and jostled over rocks and potholes. Cresting a rise a coyote stood in his way, glowing yellow eyes reflecting his headlights. It looked lanky, shoulders raised, head hanging low. It stared at him, panting, head bobbing rhythmically. It's breath visible in the cool morning air.  

Travis turned the radio down, the coyote perked its ears, lifting its head, it ran into the desert. Travis glanced at the box laying in the passenger seat before lifting his foot off the brake. The truck slowly tugged forward.  

He rolled up to the house and shut off his engine. Stepping out he stretched and yawned. Small desert birds flew about singing their morning songs. 

He got back into the truck, laying his seat as far back as it would go, and fell asleep. 

He awoke to stifling heat. The bright desert sun blinding him. Blinking and tired Travis put on sunglasses and stepped from the truck, the air smelled like campfire. He got the box from his passenger seat and walked towards the front door. He knocked but no one answered, a dog lazily barked from inside. He pictured an old dog willing to bark but not willing to stand up to do so, he chuckled.  

He set the box by the door and turned to leave. But paused, changed his mind, and picked the box back up. He put it back in the passenger seat and shut the door. From the desert someone called his name. He looked around. Carmen's mother, Rosa, came into view.  

"Travis, how are you?" she asked,  as she walked towards him she was slightly lifting the front of her dress so it wouldn't drag on the ground.  

"Hello Rosa, how are you, you look beautiful as ever", They smiled and hugged. Her perfume was soft, almost muted, but it stayed with him after she pulled away. Travis couldn't place the pleasant scent, but he liked it. 

"Thank you", she blushed and smiled at him, a shine to her eyes. "Carmen isn't home. She's in the mountains with Levi; they've been gone for days." She lifted her chin, peering off into the desert. "They should be home anytime, but maybe not", she laughed. "Come inside", she invited him. "I'm making lunch". Not waiting for a response, she looped her arm through Travis's and walked him into the house.  

Two pots sat cooking on the big iron woodstove, Rosa stirred each one, steam rising into the air. As Travis had guessed she had an old white-faced dog. A retriever lay on the floor in the corner. It wanted to bark again, lifting its head preparing to stand, but it was still lazy. It closed its eyes it went back to sleep.  

"This is Arlo", said Rosa noticing Travis looking at the dog. 

"I heard him bark when I knocked on the door, I assumed he'd be an old guy", Travis smiled. 

Rosa nodded, smiling and blinking, "yup, I don't let him wander far these days, too many coyotes about". 

"I saw one not far down the road", Travis could smell the refried beans, and the tortillas Rosa was cooking in a flat pan. His stomach rumbled. 

"Oh, I brought some gifts for everyone, I'll go get them." and he walked out the door.  

Travis was halfway to the truck when a coyote walked out from behind it. It turned its head to look at Travis. He maintained eye contact, unsure what else to do. He stopped but then slowly continued forward assuming the coyote would move on, but it didn't, Travis stopped again. "Hmm..." Travis said aloud. "What are we doing?". The coyote looked unbothered but continued to stare, tongue out slightly, panting in the heat. 

Travis started to turn back towards the house but as soon as he moved the coyote dug its paws into the dry, hard ground, as if to run. Travis couldn't tell what that could mean so he stopped moving.  

The coyote started to walk away from him but diagonally and its semi-circled back getting between Travis and the house. Closer to the truck inched up to it and hopped in. Travis honked his horn, and the coyote ran off behind the house. 

Rosa stepped out of the door, "What are you out here honking about", she yelled to Travis who was sitting in his passenger seat awkwardly squished beside the box. "Are you out here scared and honking", they both laughed. Travis pictured himself as a frightened goose, honking in the yard. 

He got out of the truck and looked around nervously, picking up the box before shutting the door. "That coyote was here", he began to explain. 

"That coyote, the same one you saw earlier?" Rosa asked with a smile, clearly teasing him. 

"Not the same one, well maybe, I don't know. It seemed to want to block my way" Travis said, heart still beating hard. 

"So, a coyote was out here playing football with you or something", She said laughing at him. 

Travis was confused, he didn't know what the coyote was doing. The old dog slowly walked out of the house and barked at him, as if he'd just arrived, then turned, dismissively and walked back indoors. Rosa watched Arlo and laughed.  

"Burritos are ready" she said, raising her eyebrows and motioning towards the house. Travis's stomach rumbled again. They walked inside; Arlo lifted his head from his pillow in the corner. 

                                                                                                                ***   
 

They walked in and Travis set the box on the floor. He pulled out a bottle of wine out of it which he handed to Rosa, she read the label as she brought it to the kitchen. He pulled out a six pack of glass bottled sodas and walked it to the fridge.  

"Would you like one of these?" He asked Rosa, she nodded, he pulled two out and closed the fridge. Using a wall mounted bottle opener, he opened them and handed her one. She took a swig and picked up her plate, Travis got his and they both walked into the living room to sit and eat. 

"So, what happened with your coyote outside?" Rosa asked, smirking. 

"I don't know, it seemed like it was confronting me?" Travis ate another bite and drank a sip of soda. "I don't know how to describe it. It stared at me. then walked around me until I honked at it". 

"Maybe it doesn't like your car?" Rosa said, Travis couldn't tell if she was just picking on him again. "Maybe it was a Bruja transformed" she said, cocking her head knowingly, raising her hands in a 'who knows' gesture. 

"Why would a witch in the shape of a coyote confront me in your dooryard?" Travis joked, poking back at her teasing him. 

"Maybe 'she' doesn't like your car?" Said Rosa playfully. Travis smiled but thought it was creepy, all the witch talk.  

He didn't believe in brujas, but he didn't like the idea of one stalking out by his truck, nonetheless.  

Arlo walked up to Travis, slow and arthritic, to beg for his last bite. Travis looked at Rosa, motioning towards Arlo with his last bite. Rosa shook her head yes. Travis reached out his hand, and the old dog carefully and gently took the last of burrito from his hand and returned to his corner pillow, swallowing the bite before laying back down.  

"Whatever it was it's strange that it was confronting you", said Rosa. This further unsettled Travis but when he looked up at her face she was wearing a huge grin. "You're not going to get scared and go back out and honk more are you?" She asked, laughing harder this time, she slapped her leg, clearly amused by her own joking.  

                                                                                                                ***   
 

As the day waned Travis started to fidget and look out the windows periodically. "When are those two supposed to return?" he asked again, clearly distracted by the idea of witch's and rogue coyotes.  

"Thinking of honking again, Travis?" asked Rosa sarcastically.  

Her joking was starting to rub him the wrong way. Why is she teasing me so much, he thought. Does she want me to leave? Is she mad that I didn't respond to her advances when I first got here? He practically grew up there, the Carmen, Levi, Travis trio. From when he was thirteen or fourteen until he was around seventeen, he was at Rosa's house nearly every day. Thinking about it now though, and somehow, he missed it earlier, ten years ago Rosa was a much frailer woman. She didn't just seem to have not aged in the ten years he had been gone; she looked younger now than when he last saw her. The witch fear struck him again and he looked at Rosa. She looked right back and widened her eyes. Travis wanted to run away. 

"You look really good Rosa, do you have some new routine or some new herbs you take?", hoping for some explanation. 

"My routine", said Rosa, looking off for a second. "Hmph", she said sounding annoyed. "Well, I wake up in the morning", and in a matter of fact tone started listing her routine, "I stretch my back, I put on my shoes, I go to the bathroom, I brush my teeth, I walk Arlo, I eat breakfast, I brush my hair, I go for a walk, I eat lunch, I walk Arlo again..." I must have looked annoyed because she stopped and asked, "do you want me to continue?" in an overly sweet voice like I was a child. 

Travis was confused, I guess I could have been being rude, he thought. I couldn't remember what face I was making. "Oh, no" said Travis "I was just thinking about how late it's getting" Travis's face turned hot and red. "Please continue" he said. 

In a dry tone she said, "Well if you're thinking about other things I'll just keep my routine to myself", sounding unamused.  

"Oh, please continue", said Travis, now sure that he must have been rude. Probably making some dramatic face in his fearful rumination. Travis now embarrassed, forgot that he had been angry a few minutes ago. "Please continue", he said again, smiling at her. 

Rosa continued, picking up in the same tone and exactly where she left off "I clean and do the dishes, I make my bed, I water the houseplants, I go to town and go shopping at the market, I come home, I walk Arlo again, I make dinner, I eat, I wash up, I go to bed." She paused, thinking, it seemed like she was seeing if she forgot anything. She sat up like she remembered something. "Oh yeah, I train my pet coyote to scare guests", she said in the same serious voice and laughed. 

Travis' eyes narrowed, he was annoyed again. She was picking on him. "I should go", he said, standing up, looking around to see if he had things to gather. 

"But what about Carmen and Levi, they should be back anytime now?" She smiled at him looking coquettish again. 

Travis smiled uncomfortably, bright pink filling his cheeks. "No, I have to get back, I thought they might not be here, they didn't know I was coming, that's why I brought these gifts, to leave in case I didn't see them". Travis realized that he was rambling, but his mind was foggy with stress, and he didn't know what to say, he just wanted to leave. While he was rambling, Rosa was circling him and now stood between him and the door. 

                                                                                                                ***   
 

Travis looked around the room as if he was just going to ignore Rosa. Her standing between him and the door was just too much for his mind to handle. He shuffled around clearing his throat, running his hands over what was in his pockets.  

"Yeah, I should really get going", he pulled his keys out of his pocket. Rosa snatched them out of his hand. Travis stepped backwards, shocked by her precision and speed.  

He took a step towards her, "Could I have my keys?" asked Travis smiling, still trying to maintain a level of decorum. 

Rosa mimicked back his exact same smile and calmly said "no", shaking her head while maintaining her smile.  

Travis didn't know what to do, they both just stood there smiling at each other. 

A bead of sweat ran into his eye, wiping at it he noticed Rosa rhythmically shaking her hips and shoulders back and forth. Travis relaxed, maybe she wants me to open that wine, he thought. He smiled, doing a little dance he looked at her face, she was snarling, teeth bared. As soon as their eyes met, she pounced on him, grabbing him by the wrists she wrestled him to the ground. Travis struggled back and forth, trying to pull his wrists away. Rosa was too strong.  

"Wait, wait, wait, we don't have to do this", said John. Trying to slow the situation down, trying to maintain even a sliver of control 

She was squeezing his wrists so hard he thought she might break them. He couldn't help it, his shot nerves gave out and he started crying. He couldn't remember ever feeling so helpless. 

Rosa let go, Travis fell back and rolled to safety. He sat up, rubbing his wrists, crouching defensively. Rosa quietly walked over to him and softly rubbed a hand on his back. He flinched away and looked at her. She laid her hand gently on his arm. She was smiling sweetly, with a warm shine in her eyes. Travis wanted to lean into her soft touch. 

"Did I scare you?" Rosa asked in a soothing voice. Then she turned harsh and condescending. "Travis, it was just horsing around, just wrestling. Don't be dramatic!" She was getting heated, her eyes suspicious slits. Little white pinpricks of light reflected back from her inky, dark pupils. She glared at Travis with a deep look of contempt. Then she walked away into the next room and slammed the door. 

As soon as she was gone Travis stood up, looking out the window, it was getting dark. The sun was gone behind the mountains and stars were starting to blink in the sky. Travis heard a loud bang that sounded like the hood of his truck shutting. In the dim light he could see the hood was closed and some wires hung loose, sticking out from underneath it. He ran out the door and three or four coyotes scattered into the surrounding scrub. He ran to his truck, hopping into the driver's seat. Putting the key in the ignition he turned it, it did nothing. He popped the hood and ran to open it up. The loose wires were the battery terminals. Something had disconnected his battery.  

He started to try and re-attach them, but it was too dark, and he thought he might need tools. Something growled from the darkness. He tried to edge around the truck to get back in the driver's door but something else growled from that direction. A chaotic chorus of high-pitched yips, yaps, and barks started. It grew in intensity; there seemed to be a large pack of coyotes right behind the truck. Travis couldn't see a thing in the darkness and ran scared, back into the house. 

Rosa was there looking sweet, playing innocent.  

Travis was distraught, "There's a whole pack of coyotes out there, did you hear that? They were very aggressive", he said, looking around frantically. 

"I made Carmen's bed up, so you'd have a place to sleep for the night", said Rosa, changing the subject.  

"Didn't you hear me, I think they disconnected my trucks battery", He stopped, realizing coyotes couldn't do that. It had to have been a human. He looked at Rosa.  

She reached out touching his arm. She pulled Travis close to her. His heart started beating so hard he thought she might hear it. Travis was scared in every way possible, too scared to react.  

She walked him into Carmen's room and sat him on the edge of the bed. "OK, you can sleep here", she said. She left the room and returned with a folded-up towel, setting it beside him. "This is for washing up, in case you want to take a shower". 

Travis, bewildered by the day's events, in a timid voice, said, "Thank you". 

Rosa smiled and rubbed a hand on his back. 

Tired, Travis closed his eyes, letting his head fall forward. He heard Rosa whispering something as he nodded off to sleep. 

                                                                                                                 ***

Travis woke up with a  pounding headache. He tried to roll to get up but his body was so sore he couldn't lift himself to a sitting position. He fell back into his pillow, so sore he thought he might have thrown his back out.

Where are my clothes? he thought, realizing he was only wearing his t-shirt and boxer shorts. The bedding was smooth and clean, freshly made. The linen smelled of wildflowers maybe, a bit like the scent he noticed on Rosa when he'd arrived. 

Spots of dappled sunlight moved across his blanket. The sunlight coming through lace curtains, gently blowing in the breeze. Travis rolled over, blinded by the sun spots coming through the curtain. He slid his legs out of bed and kind rolled onto his feet. He hunched like maybe he had a pinched nerve. The sunlight somehow made his hands look old and wrinkled covered in sunspots.

He opened the door and shuffled out into the living room. Rosa was there, cooking breakfast.

"Good morning sleepy head", she said smiling he smiled back.

"Good morning", he said. His head was a fog, he couldn't formulate thoughts. He tried to piece together his memories but all he could remember was that he was st Carmen's house. Rosa was the only one home. What else could he remember? Something about a coyote, he had some kind of nightmare about Rosa attacking him?

"I'm making us machaca con huevo", she said, handing him a cup of coffee. He took the coffee and drank a sip, it tasted rich, he savored it

"Thank you", he said, really meaning it, leaning towards her a bit, squinting his smiling eyes. Looking at her Travis realized that she looked absolutely stunning. She was maybe the most attractive person he had ever seen. He wondered if he was falling in love with Rosa, 'I deffinitelly have a crush on' her thought, chuckling to himself as he walked to the bathroom.  

He shuffled his feet into the small bathroom, he closed and locked the door. While going to the bathroom he noticed that he had more wrinkles everywhere, he seemed maybe, older. He picked up the soap and turned on the tap to wash his hands. He looked up and startled at the face looking back at him in the mirror. He was covered in wrinkles and deep lines he didn't remember having before. He thought of the weird lighting and that maybe they drank wine last night. Did they drink wine last night? he questioned. maybe I'm really dehydrated? 

The soap fumbled from his hands, it bounced into the ceramic soap dish on the vanity, sending it smashing onto the tile floor.

"Are you alright?", Rosa called from the kitchen.

Travis rinsed and dried his hands before walking out of the bathroom. "I broke your soap dish", he said, as he walked back into the living room, putting his hands together apologetically. "I'll clean it up", he walked across the room picking up the broom and dustpan. Arlo lifted his head, his white face watching Travis walk across the room

"Well, hurry along," said Rosa. "Breakfast is almost ready",

Travis cleaned it up as fast as he could but his back hurt and his arms and legs were tired, luckily his headache had subsided.

                                                                                                                 ***

Travis set his coffee down by the plate Rosa had set out for him. To be polite he waited for her to sit before he started eating. The eggs looked fluffy and perfect. Pieces of red and green pepper, little flecks of white onion, little bits of steak throughout, it looked delicious. 

Rosa sat down with her coffee. "Ah, being polite I see", she said sipping her coffee. "Let's eat!" she said with a smile and a little cheer.

John smiled and dug in. He took a bite and let out a small moan. Tilting his head he gave Rosa a doe-eyed look. He started eating faster. He started to say something but chocked on his food and he started coughing.

"Don't be such a slob", said Rosa, "Slow down and you won't choke on your food", she continued eating slow and maybe extra proper.

Travis took a swig of coffee and cleared his throat. "I'm sorry", he said. And he calmly started eating again.

Before he was finished Rosa picked up both plates and brought them to the kitchen. "wait", said Travis as  the rest of his breakfast got walked away.

"Well, playtime's over", said Rosa. She stood, chin raised, legs spread, chest held high. Travis was in awe at how strong and proud she looked, so powerful. She looked at the door. "You wanted to see them, your babysitters are back",  Travis followed her gaze. As if on cue, wind gusted hard and the door blew open, Carmen and Levi Walked in. 

                                                                                                                 ***

Carmen looked at her mother but spoke to Travis, "Travis, funny to see you here, playing house with my mother" She scoldingly stared  Rosa in the eyes while speaking.

"Looks like we should have left them to keep playing, whatever this was they were playing" said Levi, shrugging his shoulders.

Travis was confused, he went to stand to greet them but his foot slipped as he began to move and he fell right back into his seat, so he stayed there. He felt so miniscuel compared to everyone here. Rosa, fierce, beautiful. Carmen and Levi, both looked so strong and vital, carrying a kind of static charge around them, Their eyes were dark and deep and when Travis tried to look into them they were swirling with clouds or stars. They both looked so strong, so powerful, light enough to float yet heavy enough to crush stone. All three of them had changed. Travis though, thought he had only become a failure. 

"I'm leaving", Rosa announced, she walked over to Arlo and motioned for him to get up. He stood and she picked up his dog bed, she pointed towards her room and Arlo started walking towards the door. She followed keeping the slow pace behind Arlo. They went into her room and quietly shut the door. Carmen and Levi watched her perform the whole production she was putting on. 

They both looked at Travis. At first warm and smiling but then their faces turned serious. Travis looked away. Nervous, he wished he would have followed Rosa.

"Look at the trouble you've gotten yourself into", said Carmen.

"You are almost dead", said Levi, in a matter of fact tone. Carmen was  nodding her head.

"You probably think you're in love with her", said Carmen. Travis's flushed wondering if she could read his mind.

"She 'can' read your mind" said Levi, again, in a matter of fact tone.

"Don't you see it?" asked Carmen. "If we didn't show up she would have killed you, or made you her pet, and you were  more than glad to play along!" Carmen sounded harsh and serious.

"Wait, what did she do to me?" Travis was starting to fidget again, glancing around nervously.

"Rosa is a powerful bruja, doing what powerful brujas do, they steal the life from fools" Carmen paused, "you are the fool".

Travis blinked. He didn't have anything to say. He wanted to defend Rosa, he also wanted to defend himself. But non of this made any sense to him, he furrowed his brow, he was still confused.

"Are you ready to leave", Carmen asked Travis.

"Oh, my truck won't start, The battery got disconnected?",  his voice rose as if asking a question. A bit of fog started to clear.

"I'll go look at his truck", said Levi as walked out the door.

                                                                                                                 ***

Carmen took Travis's hands and gently stood him up. "Listen, if you leave now you should still survive. There's a chance you won't survive!" She grabbed him by both of his shoulders and gave him a stearn look in the eyes and said "You showed up here weak and broken, you'll be lucky to make it out alive". Her face softened and Travis drifted, lost in her swirlling dark abyss eyes. Carmen stepped back smiling and she stood upright. Travis could just feel her presence, it enveloped him. In her shadow,  he wanted to be hers.

Carmen shook her head and looked at him, head cocked, eyes squinted. She lead him out of the house to his truck. 

"Levi got it working" stated Carmen.

"Yup", said Levi

They walked him to the drivers seat and opened his door. He got in and it started with no problem.

Carmen leaned into the window and said, "you can't come back here, it's not safe for you anymore". Travis nodded his head up and down. He was frowning and awkward. He backed the truck up a little ways and turned around, he drove away, probably to never return. Side by side Levi and Carmen watched as the truck bumped and jostled over the loose dirt and stones.
