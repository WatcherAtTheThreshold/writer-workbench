# Outline

Book #1 John finds ring / Mary dies and is resurrected

Book #2 John and Mary meet / he can't "read" her / her necklace blocks his ability / She doesn't wear the necklace and he can "see" her / He confesses his power / she confesses to not seeing the "magic" around before John was in her life / she suspects John is mentally unstable  / They work on it

Book #3 The pressure is too much and they break up / they create new lives / something mystical(or cursed) breaks them out of there lives and they end up back together

Book #4 They live the lives that's described in Book #1, they buy a house, Tabitha shows up, etc. But they are clearly still coping and Johns coping starts looking more and more like a disorder


This should create a funny dichotomy in the last three books where you naturally want to root for them, for their love, but you know the outcome and you know that Johns not just insane per se, but is cursed, I guess
